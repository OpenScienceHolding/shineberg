What this place is

Luka was my closest friend. A pure soul, with big dreams for his son.

Noah was born only months ago, and he won't remember him. So we're building him one place — where his father's voice, his face, and the stories of everyone who loved him will be waiting. So that one day, when he's ready, he can come here and meet his dad.

It's being built now, together. It will keep growing as long as there is more to add.

How you can help

If you knew Luka, you hold a piece of him.

What matters most isn't what's written — it's his voice and his face. Send anything with Luka in it:

  • ·Voice notes & videos — any moment with his voice in it. This is what disappears fastest, and what Noah will want most.
  • ·Photos — from any time, even the most ordinary ones.
  • ·A memory or a story — a joke of his, a saying, a moment. The smallest and most personal is the most precious.
  • ★And if Luka ever spoke to you about Noah, about becoming a father — that is what I most want Noah to hear.
